Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Chess Openings Academy ("the App", "we", "us") is a free educational Android application that helps users learn chess openings, practice chess positions, take quizzes, and track their learning progress.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our application.

1. Information We Process

The App stores learning information such as completed lessons, quiz results, opening preferences, practice history, achievements, settings, and progress. This information may be stored locally on your device.

If online accounts become available and you choose to register, we may also process your username, display name, account identifier, optional profile image, and information needed to synchronize your learning progress.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App is free and uses Google AdMob to support its development and maintenance.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, app interactions, IP addresses, and approximate location derived from network information, subject to applicable law and consent choices.

Where required, the App provides a consent management interface. You may be able to review or update your privacy choices through the App's privacy settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Policies .

3. Accounts and Online Services

Chess lessons can be accessed without creating an online account.

Optional online functionality, when enabled, may use Google Firebase services for authentication, profile storage, synchronization, and leaderboards.

If these features are activated, relevant account information, profile details, and synchronized learning results may be processed by Firebase infrastructure.

More information: Firebase Privacy Information .

4. Public Profiles and Leaderboards

When online leaderboards are available, participating users may have their chosen username, avatar, ranking, learning level, and mastery score displayed publicly within the App.

Email addresses and authentication credentials are not intended to appear in public rankings.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • Provide chess lessons and quizzes.
  • Save educational progress and preferences.
  • Improve application stability and functionality.
  • Support optional accounts and leaderboards.
  • Display advertising subject to applicable requirements.
  • Protect the App against abuse and fraud.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell user account information to data brokers. Information may be processed by service providers supporting the App, including Google AdMob and, when activated, Firebase.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect users and services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored learning information may remain on your device until you clear application data or uninstall the App.

If online accounts are enabled, account information and associated progress may be retained while the account is active, subject to applicable retention requirements and deletion requests.

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information under our control. Where online account creation is offered, we will also provide applicable in-app and web-based account deletion mechanisms.

Deletion requests do not necessarily remove information independently retained by third-party providers under their own policies or applicable legal obligations.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the services provided. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The App provides educational chess content and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Advertising, account features, and any age-related safeguards will be configured according to the App's declared target audience and applicable Google Play and AdMob policies.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal information.

Where required, the App offers advertising privacy choices through Google's consent management interface. You may also manage certain advertising preferences through your Android device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
