Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Chess Openings Academy ("the App", "we", "us") is a free educational Android application that helps users learn chess openings, practice chess positions, take quizzes, and track their learning progress.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our application.

1. Information We Process

The App stores learning information such as completed lessons, quiz results, opening preferences, favorites, practice history, achievements, settings, and progress. This information is stored locally on your device.

You can use the App without creating an online account. On the Profile screen you may choose a display name, an avatar, a theme, and whether you want to appear on a leaderboard. Those choices are stored on your device. You may also select a profile photo using Android’s system photo picker. In the current App that photo is not sent to our servers.

Public leaderboards and cloud accounts are not currently offered. Local practice scores are not posted as public rankings.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App is free and uses Google AdMob to support its development and maintenance.

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older, including teens and adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families program.

Because the App is used by teens and by people whose age we do not know, we configure every Google ad request for teen advertising treatment and a maximum content rating of G (general audiences). Google describes teen advertising treatment as disabling personalized advertising and remarketing. We apply this configuration to all users of the App, including users of unknown age. This describes how we configure ad requests. These settings describe the restrictions we request from Google's advertising systems; third-party processing remains governed by applicable law and provider policies.

Banner ads may appear at the top of the Home and Openings screens. Full-screen interstitial ads may appear only after you tap Finish on a lesson or Continue on a formal exam results screen, and only after conservative frequency limits. Practice quizzes do not show interstitial ads. Ads are never required to unlock lessons, quizzes, or other learning content. The App does not use rewarded ads.

Learn more:

3. Information collected through advertising

When ads are requested, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use information needed to show, measure, and protect ads. This can include:

  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address (for example, city-level, not a street address)
  • Device and app information
  • Advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • App interactions related to ads, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic information about ad serving and whether ads loaded correctly

This information may be used to deliver ads, measure advertising performance, reduce fraud and invalid traffic, and operate Google’s advertising services. Because of the teen advertising treatment described above, the App asks Google not to serve personalized or remarketing ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

4. Advertising consent

Where regional privacy rules require it, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to determine which advertising privacy choices to present and whether ads may be requested. The App also uses a US state privacy message where applicable, including choices relating to the sale or sharing of personal information under relevant state laws. Whether you see a message depends on your location and Google's requirements.

If you already made a choice on a previous visit, that earlier choice can remain valid. In that case, ads may be requested using your previously valid choice while Google checks whether a new message is needed. If a new message is required, it is shown so you can consent, decline, or manage options.

If Google cannot update consent information (for example, because of a connection problem), the App may keep using a previously valid choice until a new check can be completed.

The privacy message is provided by Google. It may describe advertising purposes, including personalized advertising. Independently of the wording of that message, we still configure the App’s Google ad requests for teen treatment and a G content rating, as described in section 2.

You can later review or update those choices through Privacy options on the Profile screen when that button is shown. The availability of that button depends on the consent and privacy-options requirements reported by Google's SDK.

The App requests teen-restricted ads regardless of the consent choices presented by Google. Those choices can still matter for other advertising-related processing.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • Provide chess lessons and quizzes
  • Save educational progress, profile choices, and preferences on your device
  • Improve application stability and functionality
  • Display advertising, subject to your consent choices where they apply
  • Protect the App against abuse and fraud
  • Respond to support and privacy requests

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell user information to data brokers.

Information may be processed by service providers that support the App, including Google AdMob and Google’s advertising partners, as described in sections 2 and 3.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect users and services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored learning information, profile choices, and settings may remain on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the services provided. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13.

We do not ask for your date of birth. Because we cannot tell teens from adults, all Google ad requests—including for users of unknown age—use teen advertising treatment and a G content rating, as described in section 2.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact hic.cherkaoui@gmail.com so we can review the request.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal information.

Where required, the App offers advertising privacy choices through Google’s User Messaging Platform, as described in section 4. On Profile, use Privacy options when that button is shown. You may also manage advertising identifiers in your Android device settings.

You can change your display name, avatar, theme, and other on-device settings at any time in Profile. You can remove locally stored learning information from your device by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App, subject to any device backup or restore settings.
